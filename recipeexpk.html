Cinnamon buns: 5±2

gone too quick

doesn't keep well but that is obviously not going to be an issue

Est. prep. time: set aside your whole afternoon

Est. time before 100% devoured: ~2h

  • 5ish cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 9/2 tsp dry yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 cup dark brown sugar
  • like a decent amount of cinnamon (duh)
  • 6 tbsp softened butter
  • 7 tbsp softened butter
  • 56g cream cheese
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • 3/2 cups powdered sugar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  1. In bowl B, combine 1 cup flour with 1/2 cup sugar, the yeast and 1 tsp salt
  2. In cup C, heat combination of 1/2 cup water, 1/2 cup milk and 1/4 cup butter to temperature between 440/9°C and 490/9°C
  3. Gradually add contents of C to B
  4. Mix real good (scrape bowl while mixing)
  5. Add eggs and 1 cup flour and mix (s.b.w.m.)
  6. Add remaining flour, stop when soft dough formed
  7. Knead until smooth
  8. Cover & rest 30 min
  9. Split dough into two portions
  10. Roll each portion into flat sheet
  11. Mix dark brown sugar, decent amount of cinnamon and 6 tbsp butter (this is the filling)
  12. Spread filling onto dough (thickness of filling should be approx 1-2mm)
  13. Roll tightly without squeezing the filling all over the place. Diameter of each roll should be approx 6-8 cm.
  14. Using dental floss or whatever other cutting instrument of choice, slice into pucks of around 3-5 cm thickness each
  15. Place pucks into adequately sized pan
  16. Cover & rest 1h
  17. Bake @1715/9°C for 25-30 cm
  18. Mix 7 tbsp softened butter, 56g cream cheese, 2tbsp milk, 1/2 tsp vanilla, 1/4 tsp salt
  19. To previous mixture use whisk to mix in powdered sugar: icing complete (make more if you want)
  20. done; serve with or without icing

I prefer when the buns explode outta the pan cuz then the filling of the bun makes a crispy caramelized shell. Also it looks really goofy

Bai Tang Gao: 3

Simple recipe which does not require active attention most of the time. Not too hard to make either. It seems that the layers aren't uniform and it would be nice to find a way to propagate the texture from the top of the bai tang gao to the rest.

Est. prep. time: (3/4)h active + 3h passive + (1/3)h active

Est. time before 100% devoured: ~36h

  • 150g rice flour
  • 280g water
  • 100g white granulated sugar
  • 3g instead yeast
  • 30g lukewarm water
  • vegetable oil
  1. In pot A, mix rice flour with 80g water
  2. In pot B, mix sugar with 200g boiling water
  3. When sugar dissolved, mix B into A until smooth
  4. Dissolve yeast into 2tbsp lukewarm water (separate bowl)
  5. Add yeast to A
  6. Ferment 3-5h
  7. Pour A into glass pie dish (8-9 in diameter)
  8. Steam 10min
  9. Let rest 10min in steamer
  10. Remove pie dish from steamer and brush with oil

Carrot cake: 3

peak fall vibes

Est. prep. time: 1h active + 1h passive

Est. time before 100% devoured: 56h

  • 5/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 5/4 tsp baking powder
  • 5/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • like a tiny bit of ground cloves i guess
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 pound carrots
  1. Grate carrots into shreds
  2. In bowl A, mix flour, baking powder, cinnamon, baking soda, nutmeg, salt, cloves
  3. In bowl B, beat eggs, brown sugar, granulated sugar
  4. Gradually add oil into B while continuing to beat mixture
  5. Gradually add contents of A into contents of B while continuing to mix mixture
  6. Add carrots to B
  7. When homogenous but not overmixed, pour into 23 cm x 33 cm pan lined with parchment paper
  8. Bake @530/3°C until toothpic poked into middle of cake comes out clean
  9. done
