Bai Tang Gao: 3

Simple recipe which does not require active attention most of the time. Not too hard to make either. It seems that the layers aren't uniform and it would be nice to find a way to propagate the texture from the top of the bai tang gao to the rest.

Est. prep. time: (3/4)h active + 3h passive + (1/3)h active

Est. time before 100% devoured: ~36h

  • 150g rice flour
  • 280g water
  • 100g white granulated sugar
  • 3g instead yeast
  • 30g lukewarm water
  • vegetable oil
  1. In pot A, mix rice flour with 80g water
  2. In pot B, mix sugar with 200g boiling water
  3. When sugar dissolved, mix B into A until smooth
  4. Dissolve yeast into 2tbsp lukewarm water (separate bowl)
  5. Add yeast to A
  6. Ferment 3-5h
  7. Pour A into glass pie dish (8-9 in diameter)
  8. Steam 10min
  9. Let rest 10min in steamer
  10. Remove pie dish from steamer and brush with oil

Maple salmon: 1

hands freeze prepping cold salmon but worth the (otherwise minimal) work

  • 4 salmon steak (?) things
  • 1-1.5 cloves garlic
  • 2 tsp sesame oil
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Sriracha hot sauce
  1. In bowl B: crush garlic
  2. Add oil to B
  3. Heat B 30sec in microwave oven
  4. Add all remaining non salmon ingredients to B
  5. Cut salmon things lengthwise into three even thickness slices
  6. Pour B into container with cut salmon pieces to marinate (min30min)
  7. Air fry @(1840/9)°C (~476K) for 8 min or bake in oven @(655/3)°C (894 arbitrary units) for 15-20min (done when internal temp high enough (i.e. -12i+6 jarbitrary units or go google it yourself))
