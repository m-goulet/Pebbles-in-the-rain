Bai Tang Gao

Simple recipe which does not require active attention most of the time. Not too hard to make either. It seems that the layers aren't uniform and it would be nice to find a way to propagate the texture from the top of the bai tang gao to the rest.

  • 150g rice flour
  • 280g water
  • 100g white granulated sugar
  • 3g instead yeast
  • 30g lukewarm water
  • vegetable oil
  1. In pot A, mix rice flour with 80g water
  2. In pot B, mix sugar with 200g boiling water
  3. When sugar dissolved, mix B into A until smooth
  4. Dissolve yeast into 2tbsp lukewarm water (separate bowl)
  5. Add yeast to A
  6. Ferment 3-5h
  7. Pour A into pie dish (8-9 in diameter)
  8. Steam 10min
  9. Let rest 10min in steamer
  10. Remove pie dish from steamer and brush with oil
